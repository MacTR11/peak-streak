Peak Streak

TriPeaks solitaire with a twist: streaks.

Tap cards one higher or one lower than the pile to clear the peaks. Play them in a row to build a streak, and watch your score climb on a relaxing journey through 20 beautiful places.

Download on the App Store

  • Win streaks
  • A daily challenge
  • No adverts
  • Plays offline
  • Progress saved to iCloud
