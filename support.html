How to play

  1. Tap a face-up card that is one higher or one lower than the card on the pile. Suits don’t matter, and kings and aces go on each other.
  2. Cards played in a row make a streak. Their values add up and the total is multiplied by the number of cards, so long streaks score far more.
  3. Taking the top card of a peak adds +2 to the multiplier. Clear the board to win the level.
  4. No card fits? Tap the deck to turn over a new one. Drawing banks your streak, so play every card you can first.
  5. Clearing the board earns a star, and a high score earns two or three. Collect a souvenir from every place on the journey, and use boosters such as Undo and Wild when you’re stuck.

The full rules are in the game under Settings › Help › How to play, and the Card guide explains every special card.

Common questions

I’ve lost my progress, or I have a new phone

Peak Streak saves your progress on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud account as well. To carry on on a new iPhone or an iPad:

  1. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices.
  2. Check that iCloud is on for Peak Streak: open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, and find Peak Streak in the list of apps that use iCloud (you may need to tap to see them all).
  3. Open Peak Streak on the new device while it is connected to the internet, and give it a minute.

Progress from your devices is combined rather than overwritten, so you can play on an iPhone and an iPad.

If the game was deleted while iCloud was off for it, its progress cannot be brought back. We do not keep a copy of anyone’s progress.

How do I restore my purchases?

In the game, tap the gear on the home screen to open Settings, then Purchases › Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought with.

This brings back one-time purchases and an active VIP membership. Coins you bought are part of your progress, which moves between devices through iCloud (see above).

I bought something and it hasn’t arrived

Close Peak Streak completely (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again while connected to the internet. The App Store hands the game any purchase that did not finish, and the game delivers it.

If the game says your purchase is waiting for approval, Ask to Buy is on for your Family Sharing group. The item arrives once the family organiser approves it.

Still missing? Email us with what you bought, the date, and the order ID from Apple’s receipt email.

How do I cancel Peak Streak VIP?

VIP is a subscription through Apple, so it is cancelled in your Apple Account settings rather than in the game:

  1. Open the Settings app and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then Peak Streak VIP.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next payment. That includes a free trial, if you started VIP with one. You keep VIP until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the game does not cancel the subscription. Apple’s guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are made to Apple, so refunds are decided by Apple and we cannot issue them ourselves. To ask for one:

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.
  2. Choose I’d like to › Request a refund, and pick a reason.
  3. Select the Peak Streak purchase and submit.

Apple’s guide: Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple.

Are there adverts?

No. Peak Streak has no adverts. The game is free to play, with optional coin packs and an optional VIP membership.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Reminders about your daily gift and the weekend Streak Festival are off unless you turn them on. Change them in the game’s Settings › Gameplay › Daily gift reminders.

If you said no when iOS first asked, the switch cannot turn them on by itself: open the Settings app, go to Notifications › Peak Streak and allow notifications there first.

How do I sign in to Game Center?

Game Center is optional; you only need it for leaderboards and achievements. In the game’s Settings, tap Sign in to Game Center.

If that option isn’t available, sign in to Game Center in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad (search Settings for “Game Center”), then close Peak Streak completely and open it again.

Can I make the cards bigger, or turn the music off?

Yes. In the game’s Settings:

  • Look › Bigger cards makes the cards larger, and Four-colour deck gives each suit its own colour.
  • Sound and feel turns sound effects, music and haptics on or off.
  • Gameplay turns hints and celebrations (confetti, flashes and shakes) on or off.

Text in the game follows your iPhone’s text size.
