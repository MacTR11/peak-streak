1. Who we are

Peak Streak is a solitaire game for iPhone and iPad made by Macauley Treleaven, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Macauley Treleaven.

We are responsible for the game and for any personal data it handles (in data protection law, the “controller”). If you have any question about privacy, email us at macauleytreleaven@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Peak Streak app, its Home Screen widget and these web pages. The web pages use no cookies, no analytics and no scripts. The company that hosts them may keep the standard technical logs that any web server keeps.

2. What the game collects: nothing

Peak Streak does not collect any information about you or about how you play. It contains no analytics and no advertising software, and it sends nothing to us or to anyone else. The only things that leave your device are the ones you choose to use and that Apple runs for you: iCloud, Game Center and App Store purchases, described below.

If we ever add anonymous usage statistics to help balance the levels, we will update this policy first, and you will be able to turn them off in the game’s Settings.

3. What stays on your device and in your own iCloud

Everything you do in the game is saved on your device: your level and stars, coins and boosters, card backs and skies, daily gifts, quests and events, statistics such as your longest streak, your settings, and a record of the purchases already delivered (see Purchases).

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game also keeps a copy of this in your own iCloud account, using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so that a new iPhone or your iPad can carry on where you left off. To combine progress from two devices correctly, the game gives each of your devices a random code, which is kept in that copy too. Your iCloud data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple’s Privacy Policy. We cannot see it, and we do not keep a copy of your progress anywhere.

The Home Screen widget shows a small summary (your level, place, stars and whether today’s gift is waiting) that the game saves on your device. It is not sent anywhere.

Deleting the game removes what it saved on that device. To stop the game using iCloud, open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then iCloud, and turn off Peak Streak in the list of apps that use iCloud.

4. Game Center

Game Center is Apple’s service for leaderboards and achievements. Signing in is optional and the game works fully without it. If you are signed in, the game sends Game Center your total stars, your best streak score, your best daily challenge score for the day and the achievements you earn. Other players can see them with your Game Center name, depending on your Game Center settings.

Game Center is run by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Peak Streak does not read or keep your Game Center name, your friends list or your contacts.

5. Purchases and Peak Streak VIP

Peak Streak is free to play, with optional in-app purchases (such as coin packs) and an optional Peak Streak VIP subscription. All payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account, card details or billing address; App Store Connect shows us only sales figures that do not identify you.

When you buy something, the App Store tells the game what was bought, with a transaction number and, for VIP, when the subscription renews or ends. The game checks this on your device and keeps the transaction numbers with your progress (on your device and in your iCloud), so that every purchase is delivered once and never twice, and so that purchases can be restored.

Subscriptions are managed, cancelled and refunded by Apple. Our support page explains how.

6. Notifications

The game can remind you when your daily gift is ready, when your run of daily gifts is about to end, and when the weekend Streak Festival begins. Reminders stay off unless you allow notifications when iOS asks.

Reminders are scheduled on your device by the game itself. No information is sent to us, or to any notification server, to make them work. Turn them off in the game’s Settings › Gameplay › Daily gift reminders, or in the Settings app under Notifications › Peak Streak.

7. What we don’t do

  • No adverts. Peak Streak shows no adverts and contains no advertising software.
  • No tracking. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and the game does not use the advertising identifier. That is why it never shows Apple’s “Allow to track” question.
  • No selling or sharing. We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone, including data brokers and advertisers.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for, and the game never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • No access to your personal things. The game does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No profiling. We do not build profiles of players or make automated decisions about you.

8. Legal bases (UK GDPR and EU GDPR)

Data protection laws such as the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR apply to personal data, meaning information about a person who can be identified. Peak Streak is designed to handle as little as possible. Where any of the following does count as personal data, these are our legal bases:

  • Delivering purchases: using the purchase records described above is necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • Reminders: only with your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings.
  • Emails you send us: we use your email address and what you write to answer you, based on our legitimate interests in helping players (or our contract with you, when it is about a purchase).

iCloud, Game Center and App Store payments are services Apple provides to you under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

9. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the game.
  • In your iCloud: until you remove it, as Apple’s iCloud settings allow.
  • Emails you send us: only as long as we need them to answer you and deal with any follow-up.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the UK, the EU or California), you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you and to get a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to take it elsewhere. To use any of these rights, email macauleytreleaven@gmail.com. We will not charge you or treat you differently for asking.

In practice we hold very little about you:

  • Your progress is on your device and in your iCloud, under your control. We cannot see it or change it.
  • Game Center, iCloud and purchase records are held by Apple. You can see, download or delete the data Apple holds about you at privacy.apple.com.
  • Emails you have sent us: ask, and we will send you a copy or delete them.

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law: we do not sell or share personal information, as those words are defined in those laws, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office; in the EU, the authority in your country.

11. Children

Peak Streak is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone of any age.

Parents can use Screen Time and Ask to Buy on the iPhone or iPad to control purchases. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example in an email, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Where data is processed

We do not process any data from the game ourselves, so nothing is transferred to us anywhere.

Apple may process data for iCloud, Game Center and the App Store in other countries, as described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

13. Changes to this policy

If the way the game handles data changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the game.

Peak Streak has no adverts. If we ever decide to add them, this policy will be updated first, to explain what an advertising partner would receive and the choices you would have.
